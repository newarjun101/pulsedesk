# Development Roadmap

- **Phase 1: Project Foundation** (Current) — Monorepo, NestJS, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Redis, Docker, Auth, Workspaces, Tenant Isolation.
- **Phase 2: Team & RBAC** — Members, Invitations, Departments, Custom Roles, Permission Matrix.
- **Phase 3: Social Account Management** — Platform adapters, OAuth, Manual credentials, AES-256-GCM encryption, Capability discovery.
- **Phase 4: Social Integrations** — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Webhooks.
- **Phase 5: Publishing** — Media library (S3), Post composer, Multi-account publishing, Scheduling, Calendar.
- **Phase 6: Community Management** — Unified Inbox, Conversations, Messaging, Comments, Mentions.
- **Phase 7: Moderation** — Block/Unblock, Ban/Unban, Hide/Delete comments, Moderation history.
- **Phase 8: Customer Support** — Customers, Tickets, Public replies, Internal notes, SLA, Escalation.
- **Phase 9: Automation** — Rule engine, Notifications, BullMQ jobs, Socket.IO real-time updates.
- **Phase 10: Analytics** — Publishing, Inbox, Ticket, and Team performance analytics.
- **Phase 11: Production Readiness** — Security review, performance, full E2E tests, deployment.
